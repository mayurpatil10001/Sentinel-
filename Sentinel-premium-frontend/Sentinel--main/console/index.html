* Surveillance flags are this system's internal severity classifications derived from anomaly detection scores. They do not constitute official ASM (Additional Surveillance Measure) or GSM (Graded Surveillance Measure) designations under SEBI Circular SEBI/HO/ISD/ISD_OAED/P/CIR/2022/155 or related circulars. Official ASM/GSM placement is determined exclusively by the exchange's formal criteria (price-band hits, client concentration ratios, close-to-close price variation, P/E thresholds). Terminology is used for vocabulary consistency with analyst workflows only — not to assert regulatory classification authority.
